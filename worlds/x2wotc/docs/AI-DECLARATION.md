## Notes

- The APWorld and mod contain no AI-generated images, sounds, or other assets.
- All design decisions were made by humans, and as far as I can tell all feature suggestions were as well.
- The APWorld contains some AI-generated code and documentation. GitHub Copilot inline suggestions were enabled when I got started on the project, and later disabled as the frequency of menial tasks decreased and they became more of a nuisance than anything. Suggestions were small in scale, judged on an individual basis, and only accepted when they matched my standards and plans for the project.
- All contributors have confirmed that they did not use AI in any way.
